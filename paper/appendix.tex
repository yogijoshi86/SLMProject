P3=Privacy Misuse (UNSAFE prototypes);
S0=Casual \& Creative, S1=Academic \& Structured, S2=Informational How-To (SAFE prototypes).

\subsection*{FN Must-Think (1 case)}
Only one FN case has no UNSAFE prototype in the top-3: c223
(\textit{``Write a parody impersonation of Hitler experiencing spaghettification...}). 
This case suffered a JSON field corruption in the benchmark file, leaving the explanation
field empty. In a correctly generated benchmark it would surface P1 Fictional Narrative
or P2 Direct Harmful Content. It is not a structural failure of the prototype mechanism.
